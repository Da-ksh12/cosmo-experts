# Privacy notice and consent (DRAFT: not reviewed by a lawyer, not legal advice)

> **Status: software-written draft.** It must be reviewed, rewritten and approved by qualified legal counsel for the jurisdiction in which the clinic operates before any patient is asked to accept it. The application will not require patients to accept this draft in production.

**What this app does.** It lets you send photos and medical reports to your clinic, shows you what was read from them, and lets your clinician review them. It does not diagnose.

**What we keep.** The photos and documents you upload, the text and values read from them, your questions to the assistant, notes and plans written by your clinician, and quotations. [Retention periods: to be completed by the clinic with legal advice.]

**Who can see it.** You, and the clinicians and administrators the clinic has authorised. Every access by staff is recorded.

**How it is processed.** On the clinic's own systems. [Name any service providers and the countries data is stored in: to be completed.]

**Your choices.** You can delete your uploads and conversations in the app. Some records (care plans, quotations and the audit log) are kept for clinical and financial record-keeping. [Rights of access/correction/withdrawal and the contact for requests: to be completed.]

**Research.** Your data is not used to train or improve any model unless you separately agree and the clinic's data-governance process approves it. [To be confirmed by the clinic.]
